    Version 19 October 2026, revision 3. The Trumpkin Test is an unofficial, fan-made tool. It is not written, reviewed or endorsed by the author of the Trumpkin notes. It is built from Trumpkin's Notes on Building a Sauna, the heater notes and the ventilation notes. Read them; the reasoning matters more than the numbers.

    It handles flat and single-slope ceilings, not vaults or coves, and benches in a straight run or an L. Wall, floor and ceiling build-ups are nominal layer sums for space planning, with no allowance for shims, trim, door framing or a roof. Most heater figures come from a transcription of the heater comparison sheet from the Trumpkin notes, which is marked as approximate, for comparison only, and not an endorsement of any model. A few (Saunum, HUUM, Harvia Legend, the older IKI Original) come from makers' spec sheets. Confirm against the current manual before you frame anything. It does not check heater clearances to combustibles, electrical sizing or building code. Those come from the heater manual, your electrician and your local inspector.
